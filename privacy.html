Privacy policy

Orcus Hades · effective September 26, 2026

Orcus Hades is a private assistant used only by Orcus Auditory for the studio's own accounts. It is not available to the public.

What it can access

When an account owner connects a Google account, Orcus Hades can read that account's Gmail and Google Calendar. The access is read-only: it cannot send, delete or change email or calendar events.

How the information is used

Only to do what the account owner asks, such as finding an email or checking the calendar.

Sharing

Information from connected Google accounts is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone except the services Orcus Hades runs on to carry out the owner's requests.

Removing access

The account owner can remove Orcus Hades's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
